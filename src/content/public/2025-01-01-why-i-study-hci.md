# My Personal Interest and Potential Market-Talent Fit




## Unusual Path


There's a poem from Robert Frost that says:

"I shall be telling this with a sigh
Somewhere ages and ages hence:
Two roads diverged in a wood, and I—
I took the one less traveled by,
And that has made all the difference."

I believe the power of research on technology can change how our society works, how business operates, and how people understand the world. As AI becomes accessible to everyone, I believe we're seeing a broader integration of learning and the emergence of new subjects of study from HCI — a field that inclusively brings together social science, engineering, and natural science for a broader understanding of the interface between people and technology.


## The Future of Everyone as a Researcher

As time passes, AI will empower every single person to investigate hard problems and pursue knowledge. This is a phenomenal social change — one where we could see a flourishing of PhD-level intellectual capability everywhere. But on the other hand, will bad money drive out good?
