This is a second published post demonstrating how the _public folder works for published content.

## Jekyll Collections

Jekyll collections are a great way to organize different types of content. In this blog, we use:

- `_public` for published posts
- `_posts` for drafts

## Publishing Workflow

1. Write your draft in `_posts/`
2. Review and edit
3. Move to `_public/` when ready
4. Push to GitHub and it goes live!

This approach gives you full control over what content is published.
